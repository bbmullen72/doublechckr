doublechckr

Connect Gemini

doublechckr uses Gemini to read your attachments (text and page images) and compare them with your email.

  1. Go to aistudio.google.com and sign in.
  2. Click Get API key, then Create API key.
  3. Paste it below and click Save & test.

Pick a "flash" model for speed. The list fills in after you save a working key.

Your key is saved only in this copy of Chrome. It isn't in the extension's code and never goes to GitHub. When you attach a PDF, its text, images of its first pages, and your email are sent to Google's Gemini API to be checked.
