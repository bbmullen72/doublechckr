Catering Agreement

Between the GullHacks organizing team ("GullHacks") and Shore Line Catering Co. ("Caterer").

1. Services

The Caterer will provide meals for 250 hackers over the GullHacks weekend: Friday dinner, Saturday breakfast, lunch, dinner, and a midnight snack, and Sunday brunch. All menus include vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-free options.

2. Fees

ItemAmount
Meals (6 services × 250 guests)$5,900
Midnight snack: Old Bay fries and crab pretzels$650
Coffee and drinks, all weekend$300
Total Fee$6,850

3. Schedule

Service begins Friday at 6:00 PM and ends Sunday at 1:00 PM.

4. Payment

50% on signing, 50% after the event. Invoices are due within 30 days.
